## Privacy curation (zero tolerance, scanned before pushing)

The whole export tree was scanned for real personal data — card and
account numbers, the owner's street address, emails, phone numbers, and
third-party names (family, roommates, other people's businesses). Zero
hits in what is published. Specifically:

- **`data_exp44/` keeps the sanitized version** of the first export
  (12 units replaced by marked synthetic units — see its `SANEADO.md`).
  The local original, with real personal and financial data, never
  leaves the owner's machine.
- **`alma_sobre_claude/` corpus replaced by a synthetic example.** The
  battery's real corpus is built from the owner's actual days (plans,
  bills, amounts) and is not published. The public tree ships the full
  battery code with a 3-day invented corpus of the same schema, marked
  as synthetic, plus the numeric result files (scores only, no texts).
  `planes_v0_datos.py` / `planes_v1_datos.py` (generated instances
  derived from the real day packages) are excluded. Detail:
  `alma_sobre_claude/SANEADO.md`.
- **The personal claims ledger is not exported.** Its method is
  described in the README and its audits are cited with their counts;
  the ledger itself is the owner's private record.
- **`alma/evals/` is not exported** (it holds real finance/routine
  evaluation data), and neither are the prototype's runtime files
  (service logs, databases, pid files) nor any other local-only tree
  (`producto/`, `integracion_fase21/`, historical experiment dirs).
- **Three minimal redactions in two papers**, declared here rather than
  hidden: two incidental mentions of a job-interview company name were
  replaced with a neutral description, and one concrete
  bank-reconciliation example (a portal name and two real amounts) was
  generalized — in `preregistros/preregistro-L1-panel-grande-2026-10-16.md`
  and `preregistros/preregistro-alma-sobre-claude-2026-10-17.md`. No
  number, method or verdict in either paper is affected.
- **Excluded for size, declared:** the raw third-party model-catalog
  dumps (~838 KB each) inside the generator run folders, and the raw
  virginity-sweep dumps (~1 MB each) inside the L1 data folders. The
  snapshots, evidence files, measurements and result summaries of those
  same runs are included.

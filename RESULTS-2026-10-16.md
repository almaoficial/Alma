## Resumen (ES)

El 16/10/2026 el meta-loop de ALMA corrió tres búsquedas completas sobre su
propia configuración (mutaciones puntuales, un generador coordinado con LLM y
recombinación genética), más una sonda factorial. Ninguna produjo una
adopción. El patrón fue sistemático: todo candidato que ganaba en el panel de
entrenamiento se revertía en el panel ciego. El freno de estancamiento del
sistema llegó a 3/3 y **el loop se detuvo solo**, a la espera de revisión
humana — como estaba diseñado. La revisión midió el patrón de tres maneras
independientes y concluyó que los «ganadores» eran **ruido seleccionado**: el
panel de selección (18 réplicas) no tenía resolución para los efectos
perseguidos (certificar un efecto de +1,44 pedía ~65 réplicas). Se agrandó el
instrumento (paneles vírgenes 72/48/24, δ recalibrado de 0,2283 a 0,1141) y se
re-midieron las 58 configuraciones propuestas alguna vez: **0 de 58** superó
la selección. La búsqueda sobre este scaffold quedó cerrada con un **nulo
certificado** a resolución 72/48/24. La configuración base es el óptimo
operativo certificado sobre el alcance medido (58 de 324 configuraciones; el
cierre es por decisión asentada, no por agotamiento del espacio).

## 1. The instrument

- The scaffold has **5 parameters** — `decay`, `gate_margin`, `n0`,
  `umbral_base`, `umbral_divisor` — a space of **324 configurations**. The
  incumbent ("base") is `{0.5 · 0.0 · 4 · 1 · 3}`.
- Evaluation is a **deterministic simulator**: each replica yields a total
  regret score summed over 24 blocks (lower is better). Δ is the base's mean
  regret minus the candidate's, in total points; the δ scale is the
  per-block mean (totals ÷ 24). Same input, same number, every time.
- Adoption is gated: a candidate must beat a **noise floor δ** (calibrated
  from base-vs-base null distributions), with **no regression** on a
  held-out panel the proposer never sees, and a third blind panel (E3) as
  veto. Every proposal, measurement and verdict is written to a
  **hash-chained ledger**. A separate **confirmation panel** stays virgin:
  the official gain of any future adoption would be measured there, not with
  the number that passed the gate.
- A **stagnation brake** stops the loop after 3 evaluated runs without an
  adoption. Unlocking it requires a human token
  (`ALMA_TOKEN_HUMANO`, read from the environment at call time) — hardened
  the same day, after an external review found the unlock checked nothing.

## 2. Morning: the gate is recalibrated (δ v3)

The wired floor inherited from September mixed scales (a per-block δ of
0.5331 compared against Δ in totals). Under a pre-registered protocol —
two earlier attempts were archived by their own rules — the noise floor was
recalibrated on a fresh panel: **δ = 0.2283 (floor 5.4781 total points)**,
panels **18 train / 12 held-out**, E3 [500, 501]. The gate was rewired and
the base re-evaluated on the new panel (Δ base/base = 0.0) before any
candidate was measured. The gate was passable, for the first time, by a
real effect of the size the system had historically produced.

## 3. Three searches against the new gate — no adoption

1. **Single-point mutations (RSO), 8 rounds.** All 8 reverted in train
   (Δ_train between −3.17 and 0.00). The final candidate was the base
   itself. Brake: 1/3.
2. **Factorial screening (probe).** A 2^(5−1) resolution-V design, 18
   evaluations: **no detectable interactions** at that resolution
   (SE 1.1935, detectability threshold 2.3869); the only firm signal was a
   main effect — raising `umbral_base` worsens regret by ~3.26 on average.
   The landscape looked additive; the hypothesis that gains hide in
   parameter combinations was weakened, not refuted.
3. **Coordinated LLM generator.** The batch generation was blocked twice by
   the free tier (HTTP 429), and a responding free model produced no
   parseable batch (0/14). A pre-registered paid exception — model
   `mistralai/mistral-nemo`, hard cap US$0.01, paper committed before any
   paid call — generated the batch for a real total of **US$0.0001774**:
   11/14 valid candidates. Exactly one was positive in train: **+0.0556,
   i.e. 1.0% of the gate floor** — and it reversed in held-out (−0.7500).
   Rejected by the gate. Brake: 2/3.
4. **Crossover (recombination over the generator's batch, 24 children).**
   The most productive search in train: **6 of 24 children positive**, the
   best at **+1.4444** (26.4% of the floor). The single finalist went to
   the gate and fell by held-out regression: Δ_in +1.4444 / Δ_ho **−3.0833**.
   No adoption. Brake: **3/3 — active** (see §4).

Pattern across methods: **every train winner reversed in held-out.**

## 4. The brake stops the loop

With the third non-adopting search, the stagnation brake reached 3/3 and
the loop halted itself, awaiting human review — the behavior its
pre-registration had declared in advance. Nothing was unlocked, tuned or
re-run "until it worked". The next step was not a fourth search but a
diagnosis of the pattern, run as a separate pre-registered study while the
loop stayed stopped.

## 5. Diagnosis: the winners were selected noise

Three independent measurements, each with its reading rule written before
running:

- **D1 — split-half consistency inside train.** Of the 7 configurations
  that had ever won in train, only **3 were positive in both halves** of
  the 18-replica panel. In the winners' band (|Δ| < 0.5), sign agreement
  between halves was 0/7: a coin flip.
- **D2 — per-replica jackknife.** The crossover's best child (+1.4444) was
  **1.06 standard errors** from zero; a single replica contributed 48% of
  its positive mass, and removing 2 replicas erased it. The generator's
  winner (+0.0556) was **0.10 standard errors** — one net point-replica
  over 18.
- **D3 — train→held-out relation in the winners' region.** All 7 winners,
  measured in held-out as a declared diagnostic: **7 of 7 sign reversals**,
  correlation **r = −0.65**. In that region, winning more in train was
  associated with losing more outside it.

Quantified cause: certifying an effect of +1.44 at 2 standard errors
requires **~65 replicas; the selection panel had 18** (paired SE 0.57–1.37
depending on configuration). The gate floor was never the problem — at
5.4781 it sat at 4.0 standard errors of the crossover winner's Δ. The
bottleneck was the **resolution of the selection panel**: each search had
been faithfully selecting the favorable tail of measurement noise, which
is, by construction, the tail that generalizes worst.

## 6. L1: a bigger instrument, a closed scope, a certified null

The human review chose the lever the diagnosis pointed to first, and
pre-registered it — the document doubles as the review act the brake was
waiting for:

- **New virgin panels**: train **72** replicas [20000..20071], held-out
  **48** [21000..21047], E3 **24** [22000..22023]; calibration pool of 60.
  Virginity verified by a full-tree sweep (402 files) before running.
- **Recalibration** (same validated method): pool σ̂ = 8.5384, validation
  difference 0.0100, and the hard adoption condition — the new floor had to
  come out *below* the old one — met: **δ = 0.1141 (floor 2.7391 total
  points)**. Gate rewired; base means on the new panel: train 83.2639,
  held-out 83.5000, E3 82.8750; Δ base/base = 0.0.
- **Closed re-measurement scope**: exactly the **58 unique configurations**
  ever proposed by the four methods — no new proposers, no new batches
  (anti-fishing, declared in advance).
- **Result: 0 of 58 passed selection** (Δ_train > 2.7391). Best mark
  **+0.1528 = 0.22 standard errors** (5.6% of the floor); 9/58 positive,
  all ≤ +0.1528; median −0.8889; worst −6.5417 — the new instrument does
  register large effects, in the negative direction, so the null is not
  blindness. The historical winners at 72 replicas: crossover child
  +1.4444 → **+0.1389**; generator's keep +0.0556 → **+0.0139**; a third
  positive → −0.1389.
- A mid-run verification incident is part of the record: execution was
  halted on a suspected violation of the adoption condition; the wired
  values were checked against disk and independent recomputation, the
  suspicion turned out to be a misreading, and the cycle resumed only on
  that evidence. Both the halt and its resolution are in the ledger.

## 7. Closure

The L1 pre-registration carried its own exit clause, and it fired:
recalibration adopted ∧ closed re-measurement produced no adoption ⇒
**the search over this scaffold is closed, by that same act, with no
further signature.** The final recorded result is the **certified null at
72/48/24 resolution**: the base configuration is the certified operative
optimum over the measured scope — 58 of 324 configurations, every one ever
proposed by four distinct search methods. The closure is a settled
decision, not a proof of exhaustion: 206 configurations remain unmeasured,
and reopening this space requires new paper that changes the frame
(a different space, task or instrument). The brake remains active. The
confirmation panel was never consumed and stays virgin.

## 8. What this does not prove

Nothing here shows improvement — or its absence — on real-world tasks.
The experiments measure a deterministic simulator; the certified null
covers the measured scope at the declared resolution, not the absolute
non-existence of a better configuration. What the day does establish is
the mechanism, exercised end to end with real proposers: pre-register →
propose behind a firewall → measure paired against a calibrated noise
floor → decide on blind panels → stop when the design says stop → and,
when the winners looked wrong, measure the instrument before blaming the
search.
